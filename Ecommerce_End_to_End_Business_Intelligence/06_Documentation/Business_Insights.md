# Business Insights and Recommendations

The following findings are the business observations documented in the project's dashboard. They should be interpreted within the dataset's reporting period and filters.

## Key Findings
- Electronics contributes the largest share of sales and is also highlighted as a major contributor to profit.
- Office Supplies has the highest reported profit margin among the categories shown in the customer-retention dashboard's insight panel (11.75%).
- The North region is the leading region by sales and profit in the regional dashboard.
- Customer retention is reported as 32.31% for Month 1 and 28.94% for Month 6 in the retention dashboard. The dashboard also reports Month-3 retention of 28.55%.
- Furniture is highlighted as a profitability concern, with a reported overall profit margin of 2.90%.
- Furniture orders with discounts above 20% are highlighted as loss-making, with a reported margin of -3.88% and approximately ₹106.4K loss.
- The South region is highlighted as having the lowest regional profit margin, reported at 6.67%.
- The dashboard flags an association between higher discounts and lower profitability.

## Recommendations Shown in the Dashboard
1. Reduce or control discounts above 20%, particularly for Furniture.
2. Review pricing and discount strategy for Furniture and Home & Kitchen.
3. Protect and expand high-margin Office Supplies.
4. Investigate loss-making products and sub-categories.
5. Improve Month-1 customer retention through targeted repeat-purchase campaigns.
6. Monitor profitability in the South region.

## Interpretation Note
These are descriptive findings from the project's analysis. They indicate areas for investigation and action; they do not, by themselves, prove that one factor caused another. Validate each finding against the underlying query or measure and the selected filters before using it in a business decision.
