# KPI Definitions

This document records the principal business metrics shown in the project dashboards. The metric names below describe the intended business meaning; use the exact measure definitions in the relevant Excel or Power BI model when reproducing the figures.

| KPI | Business definition |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total sales value for the selected reporting period and filters. |
| Total Profit | Total profit generated by the selected orders. |
| Total Orders | Count of orders in the selected reporting context. |
| Total Customers | Count of customers represented in the selected reporting context; check whether the dashboard measure uses distinct customer IDs. |
| Profit Margin | Profit expressed as a percentage of sales. |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average sales value per order. |
| Average Discount | Average discount rate across the selected order records, according to the model's aggregation method. |
| Average Shipping Days | Average elapsed days between order date and ship date for eligible records. |
| Month-1 Retention | Percentage of a cohort that makes a qualifying purchase in cohort month 1. |
| Month-3 Retention | Percentage of a cohort that makes a qualifying purchase in cohort month 3. |
| Month-6 Retention | Percentage of a cohort that makes a qualifying purchase in cohort month 6. |

## Validation Guidance
- Confirm the reporting date range and active filters before comparing KPI values.
- Confirm whether order counts use distinct Order_ID values.
- Confirm whether customer counts use distinct Customer_ID values.
- For ratios, validate the numerator and denominator separately before validating the percentage.
- For retention, confirm the cohort definition, month-number convention, and eligible cohort population.
- Reconcile dashboard totals with the corresponding source table or pivot/query using the same filters.
